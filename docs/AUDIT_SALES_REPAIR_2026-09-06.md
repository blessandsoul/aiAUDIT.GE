# Sales diagnosis repair

User's renovation-business receipt exposed a weak result despite safe refusal to
sell AI. Root causes: composite business/customer contract; no individual lost-sale
case fields; generic insufficient report replacing a stage-specific plan; a model
focus switch on ad spend before pain was settled erased known conversion evidence.

## Changes

- Business is the specific product/service. Buyer identity is separate; no inferred
  homeowner/patient identity. Generic service restatements preserve known business.
- Conversion bottleneck now explores one actual lost enquiry, stopping stage,
  customer objection and follow-up. Renovation stage wording includes site visit
  and estimate. Unknown answers remain allowed; duplicate baseline question removed.
- Established conversion bottleneck cannot be displaced merely by an ad-spend
  mention. Explicit correction/new priority still permits a switch.
- Known stage-specific plans survive insufficient evidence. Report records
  objections as testimony, not proof of an above-market price or causal diagnosis.
- Concrete lost-enquiry review: source/request/price/stage/exact objection/follow-up,
  conditional qualification/offer/follow-up tests, comparable cases, no invented ROI.
- Georgian wording reviewed with georgian-grammar; native/expert validation is not
  claimed. No UI changes, scraping or lead submission in these tests.

- 70/70 deterministic tests; targeted ESLint; Next production build and TypeScript.
- Real-model adaptive replay of the supplied information, unknown for new details:
  17 turns, business confirmed from first turn, growth focus retained, process-first
  report with no product, specific experiment and labelled hypotheses.
  Receipt: artifacts/audit-check/sales-1788695540857/sparse.json.
- Separate synthetic example with actual lost quotation and no follow-up: 15 turns,
  case/follow-up retained and useful non-AI report.
  Receipt: artifacts/audit-check/sales-1788695450712/synthetic_case.json.
- First sparse run failed by switching to ads; retained as failed evidence. A new
  deterministic regression and final sparse replay verify that repair.

These cases do not establish universal diagnostic accuracy, business lift, physical
dictation quality, or a full Deep Audit. Customer unknown remains an honest gap.
